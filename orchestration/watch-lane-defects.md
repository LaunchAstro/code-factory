# Old watcher defect coverage

The tools catalogue (`TOOLS-CATALOGUE-2026-10-05.md`, a private round record)
records 17 distinct defects from the three reviews of held PR #103. This
accounts for the twelve initial defects, two additional defects in the
second review and three in the third. Broader proofs of the same defect are
included in its original row. The new command does not incorporate either
old script. Reaper retirement is #131.

`pane list` and records every invocation; the assertions require that command
and reject text, keys and close calls across stall, UNKNOWN, OK and wait passes.
