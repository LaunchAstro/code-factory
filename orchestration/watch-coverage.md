# Old checks and shared-kit coverage

Refs #129 and spec #123 story 27. This compares actual supplied old copies
with the current kit, check by check. It does not authorise retiring a
whole script because one row is covered. Adoption is at a round boundary;
retirement and skill pointers remain #131 and its real-round prerequisite.
The [acceptance replay](replay.md) supplies incident evidence, and the
[17-defect table](watch-lane-defects.md) covers the reviewed safety defects.

Sources examined: director `kick.sh` and `WATCH.md`, `merge-rate.py`,
`sol-reaper.sh`, both `deadrun-reaper.sh` and `deadrun-reaper120.sh`, both
`merged-watch.sh` and `merged-watch120r.sh`, `gauge-watch120.sh`,
`solcap-guard120.sh`, `reset-watch85.sh`, `qjam.sh` and `watchers-up.sh`.
The shared and numbered merge/dead-run copies have the same checks; the
shared copies select the orchestrator actor from an ID file.
`watchdog.py`, the pane `reap.py`, `trainNq.sh` and `wait-keyN.sh` are
**source not in hand**: their rows
come from the round tools record (`TOOLS-CATALOGUE-2026-10-05.md`, a private round record), not executable review.
