# Domain vocabulary

> **Fill this in.** The project's domain language: one entry per term, what it
> means here, and the words to avoid for it. Core identifiers use these terms.
> The `domain-modeling` skill keeps this file. The
> [example product's vocabulary](docs/example-product/CONTEXT.md) shows the
> shape; its terms are not this project's.

## A group of terms

**Term**: What it means in this project, in one or two sentences.
_Avoid_: the words that would be confused with it.
