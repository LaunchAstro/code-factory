# Holds

A PR still red at its cap ([Review rounds](../../docs/METHOD.md#review-rounds)) is **held**, never given a third round: label `held`, one `## Held` comment with a one-line reason and the review ids, a status row with its raised UTC time, no further session. Then its ladder: dispatch the split, else ask for one director read (always for money or sensitive data), whose findings hold the piece. A ceiling with unread scope is a hold, never an approval.

An owner's answer goes on the PR as `## Owner decision` with date and exact grant; lift the hold and dispatch exactly that. A hold open for 15 minutes leads every check-in with its raised time.
